## Code credits

- **Polestar** (https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar), Copyright (c) 2026 The Polestar authors, GPL-3.0-only.
  `LevelGuide\Planner.lua` ports its XP model (`addon\Polestar\Core\Levels.lua`: the level table, quest XP
  share, grey level, zero-difference and mob XP formulas, and the route XP simulation), changed for LevelGuide,
  and its step-key hash and longest-common-subsequence step matching (`Core\Engine.lua` `Own.hash`,
  `Own.matchSteps`) for keeping progress across route edits.
  `LevelLog\LevelLog.lua` carries a copy of the same mob XP formula (zero-difference, grey level, mob XP), used
  for "your target gives ~N XP" when LevelGuide isn't loaded.
  Because this code is GPL-3.0-only, the combined work is distributed under the GPL version 3 only.
  Other LevelGuide features (placement from the quest log, "not offered" marks, while-you're-here, arrow
  damping, spawn-camp circuit, server quest levels, lost quests, the travel planner in `LevelGuide\TravelPlan.lua`,
  in-hub ordering, group-quest answers, personal spot corrections (an idea from ForeverQuest, GPL-3.0), the
  route-choice card and the death-skip / riding hints (ideas from Leveldon, no licence: ideas only)) are our
  own code written after the ideas described in `research\guides_deep_re.md`.
- **Ding!** (https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ding-xp-and-leveling-guide), by moust4ki, GPL-3.0.
  `LevelGuide\Planner.lua` ports two small pieces: reading the last "N/M" of an objective's text when the
  game keeps its counts secret (`Core\Compat.lua`), and the rested-aware kills-left formula (`Core\Grind.lua`).
- **Shortest Path Forever** (https://github.com/cjber/shortest-path-forever, by cjber), GPL-3.0-or-later.
  `LevelGuide\Boats.lua` takes its boat and zeppelin timetable (`Data\Routes.lua`: each ship's period and the
  arrive / depart seconds at its stops, which it built from Blizzard's TaxiPathNode table and the CMaNGOS
  mangos-classic transport model, GPL-3.0, on CMaNGOS classic-db's measured periods). `LevelGuide\TravelPlan.lua`
  `P.BoatTimes` / `P.BoatLearn` follow its loop arithmetic (`Transport\Model.lua`) in our own code; the
  departure is learned only from your own boardings, never shared. `LevelGuide\HostileTowns.lua` lists its Horde
  flight-map nodes (`Data\Taxi.lua`, from Blizzard's TaxiNodes) that have no Alliance flight master within 400 yd,
  and `TravelPlan.lua` follows its hazard rule (`Journey\Hazards.lua`: a 150 yd circle costing 6x, notes on the
  leg) in our own code.
